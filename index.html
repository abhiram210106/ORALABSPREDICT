⚠️ Non-Clinical Academic Research Disclaimer

OralAbsPredict is developed exclusively for computational screening, cheminformatics education, and academic research. Predictions generated by this machine learning framework do NOT constitute clinical diagnoses, prescribing advice, or therapeutic guarantees. Computational predictions cannot replace empirical in vitro permeability assays (e.g., Caco-2, PAMPA) or in vivo human pharmacokinetic clinical studies.
